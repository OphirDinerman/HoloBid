Last updated: October 2026. {{SITE}} ("we") runs an auction site for Pokemon cards and booster packs. This policy explains what personal data we collect and why. Contact: {{EMAIL}}.

What we collect

  • From Facebook Login: your name and an app-specific ID. We do not receive your email, friends or posts.
  • Phone number: to verify you and, after an auction ends, to let the buyer and seller contact each other.
  • Facebook profile link or username: typed by you, shown to the other party after an auction.
  • Your activity: listings (photos, descriptions, city), bids, orders, notifications and settings.
  • Technical data: IP address and browser details when you accept the Terms, and for security and rate limiting. If you turn on device alerts, a push subscription from your browser.

How we use it

To run the auctions, verify accounts, prevent fraud and non-payment, send the notifications you ask for, and resolve disputes.

Who sees it

  • The other person in an auction you won or sold sees your name, phone number and Facebook contact.
  • Other users see your bids only with a masked name.
  • Service providers that process data for us: hosting, SMS delivery (Twilio), Facebook (login), and your browser's push service. We do not sell personal data.

Keeping and deleting data

We keep your data while your account exists. You can edit your phone number and Facebook contact in your Profile, and delete your account there (see data deletion). Deleting removes your name, phone, Facebook details, notifications and push subscriptions. Auction and order records stay, without your identity, because the other party needs them. We may keep records the law requires.

Your rights

You may ask to see, correct or delete your data by writing to us. Users in Israel have rights under the Protection of Privacy Law, and users in the EU under GDPR.

Security and age

We use encrypted connections and restrict access to the data. The service is for people aged 18 and over.

Changes

We will update this page when the service changes.

Last updated: October 2026. {{SITE}} ("we") runs an auction site for Pokemon cards and booster packs. This policy explains what personal data we collect and why. Contact: {{EMAIL}}.

What we collect

  • From Google sign-in: your name, your email address and your Google account ID. We use the email only to identify your account and for admin access, and other users never see it.
  • Phone number and city of residence: typed by you at sign-up (we do not verify them). They are not shown publicly. Only the buyer or seller of an auction you win or sell sees them, so you can arrange payment and delivery.
  • Profile picture (optional): a small square photo you upload, shown to the other party after an auction. You can change or remove it in your Profile.
  • Suggestions and votes: text you submit on the Suggestions page, shown to signed-in users with your name partly hidden (for example J***n), and your likes and dislikes.
  • Your activity: listings (photos, descriptions, city), bids, orders, notifications and settings.
  • Technical data: IP address and browser details when you accept the Terms, and for security and rate limiting. If you turn on device alerts, a push subscription from your browser.

How we use it

To run the auctions, verify accounts, prevent fraud and non-payment, send the notifications you ask for, and resolve disputes.

Who sees it

  • The other person in an auction you won or sold sees your name, phone number, city of residence and profile picture (if you added one).
  • Other users see your bids only with a masked name.
  • Service providers that process data for us: hosting, Google (sign-in), and your browser's push service. We do not sell personal data.

Keeping and deleting data

We keep your data while your account exists. You can edit your phone number, city and profile picture in your Profile, and delete your account there (see data deletion). Deleting removes your name, email, phone, city, profile picture, suggestions, votes, notifications and push subscriptions. Auction and order records stay, without your identity, because the other party needs them. We may keep records the law requires.

Your rights

You may ask to see, correct or delete your data by writing to us. Users in Israel have rights under the Protection of Privacy Law, and users in the EU under GDPR.

Security and age

We use encrypted connections and restrict access to the data. The service is for people aged 18 and over.

Changes

We will update this page when the service changes.
